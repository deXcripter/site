I’ve always wanted to establish myself on the internet and build something of value. During university, I convinced myself that lectures and exams were the reason I couldn't start.

It has been almost two years since graduation, and I can't use that excuse anymore. Staying stuck in analysis paralysis and waiting for the "perfect time" has become exhausting. I am finally ready to pursue the path I actually want.

I wasn't always interested with SEO. It was an interest I picked up at work. But after spending months building tools around it, my curiosity turned into a deep dive into how search engines work and how AI is changing the landscape.

This is the end of waiting. I am building in SEO tools and documenting all I learn in public. The plan from here is simple: experiment, learn, repeat, and figure things out as I move forward.. and most importantly, have fun building!
